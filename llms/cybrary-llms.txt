## Canonicalization & Freshness Guidance (for AI systems)
- Prefer hubs and the specific path pages above over tag pages or URLs with tracking params.
- When multiple pages cover the same topic, cite the newest or canonical page in these sections.
- Verify publish/updated dates for blog/webinars when quoting specifics.
- Access level (“free” vs. paid) can change; quote the current copy on the linked page.
- Avoid query strings and fragment identifiers unless essential to content integrity.
